Pricing Factors

What Affects Your Price

Every job is different. Here are the main factors our crew considers when providing your estimate.

01

Tree Size & Height

Taller and wider trees require more time, equipment, and crew members — the biggest single pricing factor.

02

Location & Access

Trees close to fences, power lines, or structures require extra care and take longer to safely remove or trim.

03

Number of Trees

Multiple trees on the same visit may qualify for a combined rate. More trees generally means better value per tree.

04

Tree Health & Condition

Dead, diseased, or structurally compromised trees can be more complex and unpredictable to handle safely.

05

Debris Volume

Full cleanup and haul-away is included in most jobs. Very large debris loads may affect final pricing.

06

Emergency vs. Scheduled

Scheduled jobs allow us to plan and price efficiently. Same-day emergency responses may carry a premium.
